Brand Style Guide

Pycsure

All the joy of a disposable film camera — none of the waste, none of the screen fatigue. One tap, a roll of nostalgic looks, and a laugh in your pocket.

01 — Niche & Audience

What we make & who it's for

The niche. Pycsure is a mobile retro-camera experience. One shutter tap produces a full "roll" of 12 film-style photo variants (4 filters × 3 flash looks) plus a 4-second video — finished off with meme shutter sounds and funny pose prompts. It turns a single moment into a playful, share-ready set of analog-flavored shots.

The feeling we sell. Nostalgia without the hassle: the warmth of film and the fun of a disposable cam, reborn as a tap-and-go app.

Who we create for:

  • Gen Z & younger millennials chasing the film / analog aesthetic
  • Social sharers posting candid, "unserious" content
  • Travelers, festival-goers, campers & party crews
  • The "digital-detox / dumbphone" crowd who still want fun
  • Meme-literate casual creators, not pro photographers

03 — Typography

Clean system sans + retro mono

Primary · Headlines & UI
SF Pro / Roboto — Aa Bb Cc 123

Native system sans-serif (SF Pro on iOS, Roboto on Android). Weights 600–700 for titles and actions; 400 for body. Keeps the UI fast, legible, and effortlessly modern.

Accent · Technical & retro labels
VTG1 · BW · 4K · 00:04 · /pycsure

Monospace for filter codes, counters, timers, and any "camera readout" moment — it sells the analog-device feel and adds character.

04 — Vibe

Warm, nostalgic & playfully analog

Cozy and vintage on the surface, fun and a little unserious underneath. Think faded film prints, a satisfying shutter click, and a meme sound that makes everyone laugh. Lo-fi by design, never sterile.

Nostalgic Warm & cozy Analog / film Playful Meme-friendly Lo-fi grain Tactile Effortless

05 — Reference Styles

Brands & looks we admire

  • Camp Snap — screen-free retro digicam; warm, low-fi, soft-focus "disposable film" look and friendly, nostalgic branding.
  • Dispo — the disposable-camera app energy: candid, no-preview, share-the-roll fun.
  • Huji Cam — date-stamped, light-leaked '90s film filter aesthetic.
  • Lomography / Kodak Gold — golden-hour warmth, grain, and saturated nostalgia as a north star.

06 — Do & Don't

Do

  • Lean into warm, faded film tones
  • Keep copy light, witty, human
  • Use grain, light leaks & soft focus
  • Show candid, real moments
  • Pair clean sans UI with mono accents

Don't

  • Use cold blues, neon, or pure black/white UI
  • Sound corporate or overly technical
  • Over-sharpen or over-polish imagery
  • Pose stiff, staged, "stock-photo" shots
  • Clutter the interface — keep it one-tap
